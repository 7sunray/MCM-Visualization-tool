      Quick guide

      How to use the visualizer

      1. Enter your chain. Type the dimensions, like 40 20 30 10 30, or the matrix sizes, like 40x20, 20x30. Press Enter or Apply.
      2. Play or step. Use Play, ◀ ▶ or the slider. On the keyboard, Space plays and the arrow keys step.
      3. Read the pyramid. Each circle is the cheapest cost of one sub-chain. The glowing line is the split being tried.
      4. Follow the colours. Cream is the left part, dashed orange the right part, solid orange the cell being computed. The cost table on the right fills in alongside.
      5. Get the answer. At the end, the bottom row shows the minimum cost and the best order, and the winning path lights up.
